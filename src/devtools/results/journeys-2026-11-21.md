> DEV TOOL output — not part of the handover.

Every journey (J1–J9, J6b, J11 TV Back keys) and the rapid-input test ran against the React build on all 35
simulated devices plus *No simulation*. The six prototype scenarios ran on each too.

on screen, not covered, combined opacity ≥ 0.5, and no console errors.
